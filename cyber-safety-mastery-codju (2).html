Concept 1 of 6
🌐 What is the Internet?
The internet is a giant global network connecting millions of computers, phones, and devices worldwide.

It lets us share information, communicate, shop, learn, and play — but it also has risks if we are not careful.
🌐 How Do We Use the Internet?
We use the internet every single day:
📧
Email
Sending messages
🎮
Gaming
Online multiplayer
📱
Social Media
Connecting people
🛒
Shopping
Buying online
👣 What is a Digital Footprint?
A digital footprint is all the data you leave behind when using the internet.

Every time you go online, you leave a trail — even when you think you are just browsing quietly!
👣 What Makes Up Your Footprint?
📸 Photos and videos you post

💬 Comments and messages you write

🎮 Games you play online

🌐 Websites you visit

⚠️ It never truly disappears — even deleted posts can be seen by others!
🔒 What is Personal Data?
Personal data is information that identifies YOU specifically.

Never share these online with strangers!
👤 Full Name
Your complete name
🏠 Address
Where you live
📱 Phone
Your mobile number
🏫 School
Name of your school
🎂 Birthday
Your date of birth
🔑 Password
Account passwords
✅ Safe Online Rules (Part 1)
🟢 Use a strong, unique password for every account

🟢 Never share your address or phone number online

🟢 Only accept friend requests from people you know in real life
✅ Safe Online Rules (Part 2)
🟢 Tell a trusted adult if something online makes you uncomfortable

🟢 Log out of accounts after use on shared computers

🔴 Never meet someone you only know from the internet without a parent
⚠️ Unsafe Actions — Watch Out! (Part 1)
🔴 Sharing your school name, address, or photo with strangers

🔴 Clicking on pop-up ads that say "You've WON a prize!" — always a scam!

🔴 Downloading apps or files from unknown, unofficial websites
⚠️ Unsafe Actions — Watch Out! (Part 2)
🔴 Responding to messages from unknown numbers claiming to be from banks

🔴 Using the same password for all your accounts

🔴 Sharing your location with people you don't know in real life
📚 Key Vocabulary

Passwords & Account Protection
5 concepts · Use Next to go through each one
Concept 1 of 8
🔑 What Makes a Strong Password?
A strong password protects your accounts from hackers.

It must be long, mixed, and unpredictable. Avoid using your name, birthday, or the word "password"!
🔑 Password Requirements
Every strong password needs all four of these:
8️⃣
8+ Characters
Longer = stronger
🔠
Mixed Case
UPPER + lower
🔢
Numbers
Include 123
❗
Symbols
!@#$%^&*
💀 How Hackers Steal Passwords (Part 1)
🔴 Brute Force: Software tries thousands of combinations per second

🔴 Phishing: Fake websites steal your password when you type it

🔴 Keylogging: Malware secretly records every key you press
💀 How Hackers Steal Passwords (Part 2)
🔴 Shoulder Surfing: Someone physically watches you type

🔴 Data Breach: A website gets hacked and all passwords are leaked

🔴 Social Engineering: Tricking you into giving your password willingly
📱 Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)
2FA adds an extra security layer beyond just your password.

1️⃣ Enter your password
2️⃣ A 6-digit code is sent to YOUR phone
3️⃣ Enter that code to log in
📱 Why 2FA is So Powerful
Even if a hacker steals your password, they cannot log in without your phone!

Always enable 2FA on:
📧 Gmail & Email
📱 Instagram & Social Media
🏦 Banking and payment apps
⚔️ Weak vs Strong Passwords
❌ Weak✅ Strong
rohit123R0h!t@Sec#2024
passwordP@$$w0rd!X9#
123456Cr!cket⚡W!ns77
💡 Passphrase Method
"I love cricket and scored 50 runs!" → ILc&s50R! Easy to remember, hard to crack!
🛡️ Password Safety Rules
✅ Use a different password for every app

✅ Never share your password with anyone

✅ Enable 2FA on all important accounts

✅ Never save passwords on shared computers

Phishing & Online Scams
4 concepts · Use Next to go through each one
Concept 1 of 7
🎣 What is Phishing?
Phishing is when cybercriminals send fake emails, SMS, or messages pretending to be trusted organisations.

Their goal: trick you into clicking a fake link and entering your passwords or personal data.
🎣 Why is it Called Phishing?
The word comes from "fishing" — they cast a wide net hoping someone takes the bait.

India loses thousands of crores every year to phishing scams. It can happen to anyone — including students!
📱 Types of Phishing (Part 1)
📧 Email Phishing: "Your PAN card is expiring — click here"

📱 SMS / Smishing: Fake bank messages with suspicious links

📞 Voice / Vishing: Fake "bank officer" calls asking for OTP
📱 Types of Phishing (Part 2)
💬 WhatsApp Phishing: "Congratulations! You won ₹25 lakh in KBC!"

🎮 Gaming Phishing: "Claim your free UC/V-Bucks here!"

🏦 Job Scams: "Work from home and earn ₹50,000/month — pay ₹500 to apply"
🔍 Red Flags in Fake Messages
🚩 Urgency: "Act NOW or account CLOSED in 2 hours!"

🚩 Suspicious URLs: "sbi-india.xyz" not "sbi.co.in"

🚩 Grammar mistakes anywhere in the message
🔍 More Red Flags
🚩 Asks for your OTP, password, CVV, or Aadhaar

🚩 Too-good-to-be-true offers — free iPhone, lottery win

🚩 Greets you as "Dear Customer" instead of your name
✅ What to Do If You Receive One
✅ Do NOT click any link

✅ Do NOT share OTP or bank details with anyone

✅ Call 📞 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in

✅ Tell your parents immediately

Concept 1 of 7
👤 What is Catfishing?
Cybercriminals create fake social media profiles using stolen photos to befriend children and teenagers.

They slowly gain trust — then ask for personal information, photos, or money.
👤 How to Spot a Fake Profile
⚠️ No mutual friends

⚠️ Account was recently created

⚠️ Photos look too perfect or model-like

⚠️ Quickly asks to move to private chat or WhatsApp
😢 What is Cyberbullying?
Cyberbullying is using digital technology to repeatedly harm or intimidate someone.

💬 Mean or threatening messages

📢 Spreading false rumours online

📸 Sharing embarrassing photos without permission
⚖️ Cyberbullying is a Crime
Under India's IT Act, cyberbullying is punishable with imprisonment.

🚫 Excluding someone from online groups

🎮 Targeting someone in multiplayer games

If it happens to you — screenshot, block, and tell a trusted adult immediately.
🎮 Gaming Scams (Part 1)
🕹️ Free V-Bucks/UC: Sites claiming free currency steal your account

🕹️ Fake mods/hacks: Downloads that secretly install malware

🕹️ "Pro Player" boosts: Pays for rank boost then blocks you
🎮 Gaming Scams (Part 2)
🕹️ Gift card demands: Online "friends" ask you to buy & share gift codes

🕹️ Fake tournaments: Ask for registration fees, then disappear

⚠️ Golden Rule: Free in-game currency offers are ALWAYS scams!
✅ Social Media & Gaming Safety
✅ Keep accounts Private — only accept known friends

✅ Report and block bullies immediately

✅ Screenshot evidence before blocking

✅ Tell parents if online "friends" ask to meet in person

Day 5 · Read First
Banking Fraud & What To Do If Scammed
5 concepts · Use Next to go through each one
Concept 1 of 8
📲 OTP Scams — Most Common in India
A fake "bank officer" calls saying your account has a problem and asks for the OTP sent to your phone.

YOUR OTP = YOUR MONEY. NEVER share it with anyone!
📲 Why Banks Never Ask for OTP
Real bank employees NEVER ask for OTP on a phone call — it's against bank policy worldwide.

If anyone — even someone claiming to be from your bank — asks for your OTP, hang up immediately!

Then call your bank's official number from the back of your card.
💸 UPI Collect Request Trap
Scammers send a "Collect Request" on PhonePe or GPay saying:

"I'm sending you ₹5,000 — just approve this request."

But approving a COLLECT request means money LEAVES your account!
💸 How to Receive UPI Money Safely
✅ To RECEIVE money: Just share your UPI ID or phone number

❌ COLLECT REQUEST: Always means money goes OUT

❌ SCAN QR CODE: Always means money goes OUT

You never need to do anything special to receive UPI payments!
📷 QR Code Scam — The Golden Rule
Scammers say: "Scan this QR code to RECEIVE payment."

This is always a LIE. In UPI, scanning a QR code = paying money OUT.

🚫 To RECEIVE money, you NEVER scan a QR code!
INDIA'S NATIONAL CYBER CRIME HELPLINE
📞 1930
Available 24/7 · cybercrime.gov.in
⚡ The 60-Minute Golden Window
Call 1930 within the FIRST 60 MINUTES of being scammed.

This gives the best chance of freezing the fraudster's account before they withdraw the money!
✅ What To Do If Scammed (Steps 1–3)
1️⃣ Don't panic — act quickly

2️⃣ Call 1930 immediately — within the first hour

3️⃣ Call your bank to block further transactions
✅ What To Do If Scammed (Steps 4–6)
4️⃣ Report at cybercrime.gov.in

5️⃣ Screenshot all evidence — messages, call logs, receipts

6️⃣ Tell your parents — never hide it out of embarrassment

"Hello, am I speaking with Mrs. Sharma? I am calling from ICICI Bank fraud prevention department. We have detected an unauthorized transaction of ₹15,000 from your account."
